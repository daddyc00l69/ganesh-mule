## Why Real Honey Matters
Three simple principles that separate true raw forest honey from supermarket syrup.

### 1. Wild Canopy Foraging
Our honey is collected from wild indigenous cliff bees foraging on natural forest blooms. Hives are never artificially fed sugar syrup, antibiotics, or commercial chemicals.

### 2. Cold Extraction (Never Heated)
Commercial brands heat honey up to 75°C to speed up packaging, which denatures delicate digestive enzymes. Ours is extracted cold, retaining 100% active diastase and live pollen.

### 3. Bottled in Solid Glass
We strictly reject plastic squeeze bottles. Food-grade heavy glass is completely inert, prevents chemical leaching, preserves aromatic notes, and keeps honey pure forever.
